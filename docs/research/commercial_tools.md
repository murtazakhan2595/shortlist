# Commercial AI job-search and job-application tools (as of 2026-10-14)

Source-quality legend used below:
- **[V]** = the vendor's own site/blog (marketing)
- **[C]** = review/comparison written by a company that sells a competing job tool (Nox/noxjobs, FastApply, Resumly, Scale.jobs, JobHire.ai, Wobo, Scoutify, LangHire, Careerflow blog, Jobgether, Jobity, Outapply, RemoteJobAssistant, 6figr, Dreamwork). Treat as biased; their numbers often conflict.
- **[I]** = independent (Trustpilot pages fetched directly, press such as Semafor/BleepingComputer, Blind).
All prices are USD unless noted. "Seen" means the date I checked or the date the source states. Most vendors would not serve a pricing page to a fetch tool (Simplify 404, Teal 403, Jobright 404), so many prices are third-party reports, labelled as such.

## Q1. What each product does, how it gets jobs, whether it submits, and what it costs

### Takeaway
The market splits into three groups. (a) Autofill/tracker copilots, where you still press Submit: Simplify, Teal, Huntr, Careerflow, JobWizard, Jobright (mostly). (b) Auto-apply bots that submit for you: JobCopilot, LazyApply, LoopCV, Sonara, Massive, AIApply's Auto-Apply add-on, Nox. (c) Resume builders such as Kickresume. Typical paid tiers cost $24-40/month, and weekly plans of $13-20 are common. None of the products verified here has a published, confirmed focus on candidates in Pakistan.

### Cited Findings

**Simplify (Copilot / Simplify+)**: autofill extension plus tracker
- Free Copilot extension autofills applications. Simplify+ costs $39.99/month, $19.99/week, or $89.99 per 3 months, according to third-party reviews that checked prices between March and June 2026 [C] — [noxjobs review](https://noxjobs.com/compare/simplify-jobs-review-auto-apply-doesnt-mean-what-you-think); Nox re-checked the same figures on 2026-09-27 [C] — [noxjobs ranking](https://noxjobs.com/compare/best-ai-auto-apply-tools-ranked-honest-comparison-2026)
- Simplify+ adds AI resume tailoring, AI cover letters and AI answers to custom application questions. The free tier gives autofill and basic keyword suggestions [C] — [aceapp review](https://aceapp.ai/blog/simplify-review); [jobhire.ai review](https://jobhire.ai/blog/simplify-jobs-review)
- Copilot does not submit for you. A separate "Autopilot" mode that does submit is in beta for some paid members. Simplify says it autofills on "100,000+" career sites [C] — [noxjobs ranking](https://noxjobs.com/compare/best-ai-auto-apply-tools-ranked-honest-comparison-2026)
- Reviewers say payments are non-refundable once the subscription starts and there is no free trial. One reviewer calls a "3 months free" offer misleading [C] — [fastapply review](https://blog.fastapply.co/is-simplify-jobs-legit-2026-review); [noxjobs](https://noxjobs.com/compare/best-ai-auto-apply-tools-ranked-honest-comparison-2026)
- Reviewers say the AI resume output needs a lot of manual editing [C] — [remotejobassistant review](https://www.remotejobassistant.com/blog/simplify-jobs-review)

**Teal**: resume builder plus job tracker (Chrome clipper)
- The free tier includes unlimited resumes and unlimited job tracking, with limited keyword matching and AI credits. Teal+ was reported at $13 per 7 days, $29 per 30 days, or $79 per 90 days. Another source says $9/week, $29/month, or $179/year. The two sources conflict, and teal's pricing page returned 403 when I fetched it on 2026-10-14 [C] — [resumeup Teal alternatives](https://resumeup.ai/blog/teal-alternatives); [jobscan roundup](https://www.jobscan.co/blog/best-ai-resume-builders/)
- I found no evidence of auto-apply. Teal is a tracker and resume tool.

**Huntr**: tracker plus autofill plus resume tailoring (pricing page fetched directly 2026-10-14) [V]
- Free tier: unlimited base resumes, 2 job-tailored resumes and 2 "application packets", tracker up to 100 jobs, Chrome job clipper, map view, unlimited contact management, **unlimited application autofills**, and a small number of AI credits — [huntr.co/pricing](https://huntr.co/pricing)
- Pro costs $40/month, $90 per quarter ($30/month), or $160 per 6 months ($26.66/month). It adds unlimited tailoring and AI cover letters, a full AI resume rewrite, advanced match scoring, unlimited tracking and "advanced job search metrics" — [huntr.co/pricing](https://huntr.co/pricing)
- The pricing page does not mention auto-submit — [huntr.co/pricing](https://huntr.co/pricing)

**Jobright**: AI job matching feed plus autofill. The "Agent" auto-apply is beta or waitlisted.
- Turbo was reported at $39.99/month (up from $29.99), $17.99/week, or $89.99/quarter. There is no public pricing page. Reviewers report no free trial, no refunds, a countdown timer at checkout, and annual plans that auto-renew [C] — [jobity review](https://jobity.io/blog/jobright-review); [outapply pricing](https://outapply.com/blog/jobright-ai-pricing)
- In one hands-on test the autonomous AI Agent showed a waitlist with a paid skip-the-line option. The part that worked was autofill, which hands the form back for the user to submit [C] — [jobity review](https://jobity.io/blog/jobright-review). Wobo calls the autonomous layer "beta-stage", often routed through third-party job boards [C] — [wobo review](https://www.wobo.ai/blog/jobright-review/)
- Several reviewers praise the matching quality [C] — [wobo](https://www.wobo.ai/blog/jobright-review/); [hiredradar](https://hiredradar.com/jobright-ai-review/) (3.4/5)
- **Jobright says it covers the US market only**: "Right now, Jobright focuses on the U.S. market—complete with an H-1B visa job filter... gearing up to expand globally soon" [V] — [Jobright blog](https://jobright.ai/blog/is-jobright-legit/). Jobs are aggregated "from leading boards, company career sites, and public listings" [V] (same page)

**JobCopilot**: auto-apply bot (France-based)
- Its pricing page, fetched 2026-10-14, shows Premium "from $0.93/day" (1 copilot, up to 20 daily job matches, automated applications, tracker, AI resume/cover letter/mock interview) and Elite "from $1.05/day" (3 copilots, up to 50 daily matches, resume tailoring per application, credits to contact hiring managers). Weekly, monthly and quarterly plans exist, but the page gives no amounts for them [V] — [jobcopilot.com/pricing](https://jobcopilot.com/pricing/)
- Third-party figures conflict: about $29.99/month Basic and about $39.99/month Premium, versus $19.90/month elsewhere [C] — summarized via [langhire](https://langhire.org/blog/best-ai-auto-apply-tools-2026/), [jobhire.ai](https://jobhire.ai/blog/best-ai-auto-apply-tools)
- JobCopilot says it applies on company career pages and ATSs, covers "500,000+ companies worldwide" and works in 7 languages [V, relayed by C] — [noxjobs ranking](https://noxjobs.com/compare/best-ai-auto-apply-tools-ranked-honest-comparison-2026). In a Trustpilot reply, the company says it sources jobs from career pages and ATSs [I/V] — [Trustpilot](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/jobcopilot.com)
- Refund terms: a refund only if the user reports, within the first 7 days, a technical issue that prevents use [C, quoting ToS] — [dreamworkhq review](https://www.dreamworkhq.com/blog/jobcopilot-review)

**LazyApply**: mass auto-apply extension
- $99/yr Basic (15 applications/day), $149/yr Premium (150/day), $999/yr Ultimate (1,500/day), billed yearly. It works on job boards such as Dice, Indeed and ZipRecruiter. There is no free tier, and it claims a 30-day refund [C, checked 2026-09-27] — [noxjobs ranking](https://noxjobs.com/compare/best-ai-auto-apply-tools-ranked-honest-comparison-2026)
- It uses the same resume for every application, with no tailoring [C] — via [langhire](https://langhire.org/blog/best-ai-auto-apply-tools-2026/)

**LoopCV**: auto-apply "loops" plus automated recruiter emails
- It pulls new postings from LinkedIn, Indeed and 30+ other boards, matches them to your CV, and either applies or **emails the recruiter**. Its own copy says it will "automatically find the email of the recruiter in a company" and use a template [V, relayed] — [scoutify review](https://scoutify.com/blog/loopcv-review/); LoopCV also markets an "apply with Claude" page [V] — [loopcv.pro/apply-with-claude](https://www.loopcv.pro/apply-with-claude/)
- The free Basic plan gives 1 job-title search, up to 10 applications or emails per month and 3 boards, with no card required. Reports of paid tier prices conflict: €9.99-29.99/month, $19.99/month Standard, or about $59.99/month Premium [C] — [scoutify](https://scoutify.com/blog/loopcv-review/); [resumly](https://resumly.ai/answers/loopcv-review); [6figr](https://6figr.com/blog/loopcv-review-is-it-worth-your-money-in-2026-631)
- Coverage is described as "Global (but primarily EU-focused)" [C, secondhand]. I found no official list of supported countries.

**Sonara**: auto-apply. The original company shut down in 2024 and the product was relaunched under BOLD.
- A $2.95 14-day trial auto-renews at $23.95 every 4 weeks (13 charges a year), or $71.40 a year [C, third-party, Aug 2026] — [noxjobs](https://noxjobs.com/compare/best-ai-auto-apply-tools-ranked-honest-comparison-2026)
- "Original company shut in 2024, relaunched under BOLD". Recent reviews cite poor matches [C] — same source. I did not verify the shutdown and acquisition from a primary or press source.

**Massive (usemassive)**: daily auto-apply agent
- Pricing is not published. A third party reports about $49-99/month. It submits daily, offers visa-sponsorship filters and has "weak reviews" [C] — [noxjobs](https://noxjobs.com/compare/best-ai-auto-apply-tools-ranked-honest-comparison-2026). I could not reach its site or any independent review.

**AIApply**: resume, cover letter and interview tools, plus a separately billed Auto-Apply
- Its pricing page, fetched 2026-10-14, lists Premium monthly or annual. Auto-Apply credits are sold in packs (for example 100 or 250). No USD amounts were shown. There is a limited free tier. Refunds are "case by case", often prorated or as credit [V] — [aiapply.co/pricing](https://aiapply.co/pricing)

**Careerflow**: LinkedIn profile optimization, tracker, AI tools
- Premium was reported at $23.99/month or $172.99/year, and Premium Plus at $44.99/month or $299.99/year. Jobscan notes that its pricing "has varied across sources" [C] — [jobscan](https://www.jobscan.co/blog/best-ai-resume-builders/)

**JobWizard**: autofill extension
- The free tier has core autofill and basic match scoring. Premium pricing is "not fully transparent" [C] — via [toolworthy](https://www.toolworthy.ai/blog/best-ai-job-application-tools)

**Kickresume**: resume and cover letter builder
- $24/month billed monthly, or $8/month billed annually ($96/year), with a 14-day money-back guarantee. It claims a built-in job tracker, which another review disputes [V] — [Kickresume help center](https://www.kickresume.com/en/help-center/alternative-to-teal/)

**Newer entrants and AI-platform features (2025-2026)**
- **Nox** (noxjobs): $69/month for 300 credits or $19/week for 70 credits. It auto-submits on employer career pages, can ask the user to approve each submission, and focuses on the US job market [V] — [noxjobs](https://noxjobs.com/compare/best-ai-auto-apply-tools-ranked-honest-comparison-2026)
- **Adzuna ApplyIQ**: free auto-apply that works only on Adzuna's own listings (Oct 2025 blog, older) — via [noxjobs](https://noxjobs.com/compare/best-ai-auto-apply-tools-ranked-honest-comparison-2026)
- **ChatGPT "Jobs"**: first seen in testing in January 2026 for exploring roles, improving resumes and planning careers [I] — [BleepingComputer](https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/artificial-intelligence/chatgpt-tests-a-new-feature-to-find-jobs-improve-your-resume-and-more/). A September 2026 explainer says it is still in internal testing with no public release date [C: Careerflow blog] — [careerflow](https://www.careerflow.ai/blog/chatgpt-jobs-feature). A third-party "Jobs by Wizehire" app launched inside ChatGPT in April 2026 [V press release] — [PR Newswire](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/wizehire-launches-jobs-app-in-chatgpt-as-job-search-moves-from-job-listings-to-conversations-302732259.html). OpenAI launched ChatGPT Work on 2026-07-09. It is general workplace automation, not a job-search product [I] — [BNN Bloomberg](https://www.bnnbloomberg.ca/business/artificial-intelligence/2026/07/09/openai-launches-chatgpt-work/)
- **Claude**: I found no official Anthropic job-search product. There are open-source agents that run in Claude Code and similar CLIs, for example **career-ops**, which scans boards, scores each job 1-5 against your CV, tailors the resume and cover letter, tracks applications, and leaves the "you press Submit" step to you — [GitHub career-ops](https://github.com/santifer/career-ops/); [job-applier-agent](https://github.com/theaayushstha1/job-applier-agent)

### Inferences
- The auto-apply tools (JobCopilot, LazyApply, LoopCV, Sonara, Massive) all optimize for volume and submit for you. That conflicts directly with Shortlist's rule of drafting but never sending. The copilot tools (Simplify, Huntr, Teal) are closer to Shortlist's philosophy.
- Huntr's free tier, with unlimited autofill, a 100-job tracker and unlimited contacts, is the best no-cost complement to Shortlist. Simplify's free Copilot is the other one.
- LoopCV is the only product found that does recruiter email outreach. It sends the emails itself from templates, which is the opposite of Shortlist's fact-checked Gmail drafts.

### Gaps
- I could not fetch official prices for Simplify, Teal, Jobright, Massive, Careerflow, JobWizard, Sonara or LoopCV (403/404 or not published). Their figures are third-party and often conflict.
- I did not find official data/privacy terms for any product. Huntr's pricing page and JobCopilot's ToS refund clause were the only terms surfaced. A follow-up should read each privacy policy, especially on resale of resume data and on storing ATS credentials.
- I did not verify Sonara's 2024 shutdown and BOLD relaunch with primary or press sources.
- I found no Gemini or Google built-in job-search agent in this pass. I did not research this specifically.

## Q2. Independent evidence of results and problems (reviews, bans, spam, refunds, reply rates)

### Takeaway
The independent evidence is thin and mostly negative for auto-apply bots. On Trustpilot both JobCopilot and LazyApply score 2.1/5, with complaints about refunds, irrelevant jobs and zero interviews after hundreds of applications. Ban figures for LinkedIn automation circulate mainly through competitors' blogs, but LinkedIn's terms and documented enforcement make the risk real.

### Cited Findings
- **JobCopilot Trustpilot (fetched 2026-10-14)**: 2.1/5, 98 reviews (63 in the last 12 months), 55% one-star and 29% five-star. Themes: refund disputes, charges after short use, irrelevant jobs ("applies for unsolicited job roles", Sep 20 2026), pay below the user's stated floor, one user with nearly 600 applications and no interviews, and spam after activating a profile. Examples: "They have just taken my money for second month" (Oct 3 2026); "I hadn't received a single interview request" (Jul 16 2026) [I] — [Trustpilot JobCopilot](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/jobcopilot.com). Older third-party snapshots gave 3.0 from 106 reviews and 3.8 from 131 reviews [C], so the score has fallen or the snapshots differ — [dreamworkhq](https://www.dreamworkhq.com/blog/jobcopilot-review); [fastapply](https://blog.fastapply.co/jobcopilot-review-2026)
- **LazyApply Trustpilot (fetched 2026-10-14)**: 2.1/5, 110 reviews (only 9 in the last 12 months), 58% one-star. Themes: autofill failures and wrong data, support that does not respond, refunds delayed or ignored, a Gmail-only restriction not disclosed before purchase, results stopping, plan limits reduced. "I have never been able to use their service" (Jun 25 2026). The company does not reply to reviews [I] — [Trustpilot LazyApply](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/lazyapply.com)
- A Reddit user reportedly auto-applied to 14,000+ positions with LazyApply and got mass rejections and ATS spam flags [C, secondhand; I did not find the original Reddit thread] — via [langhire](https://langhire.org/blog/best-ai-auto-apply-tools-2026/)
- **AIApply**: a one-star Trustpilot review reports a cancellation and refund request on Oct 10-11 that was refused because the "$50 was already spent". The overall rating was cited at about 4.3 [I, via search snippet] — [Trustpilot AIApply](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/aiapply.co)
- **LoopCV**: Trustpilot was reported at 3.9/5 from 131 reviews. Complaints describe loops that found few real matches or sent nothing, and a gap between jobs matched and jobs applied [C, secondhand] — [scoutify](https://scoutify.com/blog/loopcv-review/); [resumly](https://resumly.ai/answers/loopcv-review)
- **Jobright**: Trustpilot was cited at about 4.6-4.8. Wobo notes the score was built almost entirely in the four months after Jobright claimed its profile on a paid plan [C] — [wobo](https://www.wobo.ai/blog/jobright-review/)
- **Sonara**: one source claims a "25-40% silent submission failure rate" [C, unverified] — via [langhire](https://langhire.org/blog/best-ai-auto-apply-tools-2026/)
- **LinkedIn bans**: LinkedIn's User Agreement (section 8.2) prohibits bots and automated access, and LinkedIn says such users "risk having their accounts restricted or shut down" — relayed by [scoutify](https://scoutify.com/blog/linkedin-auto-apply/) [C]. Semafor documented LinkedIn restricting a user who promoted automation tools [I] — [Semafor 2024-09-12, older](https://semafor.com/article/09/12/2024/linkedins-have-nots-and-have-bots). The claim that "23% of LazyApply users restricted within 90 days" traces back to Scale.jobs, a competitor, and is unverified [C] — [techloy](https://www.techloy.com/jobcopilot-alternatives-ai-job-search-engines-edit-before-submit/)
- On a Blind thread about LazyApply-style tools, replies mostly argue that referrals and networking beat volume [I, anecdotal] — [Blind](https://www.teamblind.com/post/ai-tools-to-automate-job-hunting-afkh0pcl)

### Inferences
- Independent signals point the same way: volume auto-apply yields low or zero interviews, irrelevant applications, and billing friction. This supports Shortlist's low-volume, high-fit, human-sends design.
- The common billing complaints (weekly plans that renew, no refunds, 4-week billing cycles) are a cost risk for a buyer in Pakistan who pays in USD by card.

### Gaps
- I found no Hacker News threads, original Reddit threads or G2 data in this pass. Reddit evidence is only secondhand through competitor blogs.
- No product publishes audited reply or interview rates. Vendor success claims could not be verified.

## Q3. Does any product cover the Pakistan-specific needs?

### Takeaway
No. None of the products reviewed handles LinkedIn recruiter posts with email addresses or Google Forms, filters for "open to Pakistan, excludes India", or applies a USD salary floor for a candidate outside the US. Jobright is US-only by its own account, and Nox targets the US.

### Cited Findings
- Jobright: "Right now, Jobright focuses on the U.S. market" [V] — [Jobright blog](https://jobright.ai/blog/is-jobright-legit/). Its listings are "limited to US listings, there is no international job database" [C] — [Jobgether comparison](https://jobgether.com/blog/jobright-vs-jobgether-senior-remote-job-search)
- Nox: "US job market focus" [V] — [noxjobs](https://noxjobs.com/compare/best-ai-auto-apply-tools-ranked-honest-comparison-2026)
- JobCopilot claims 500,000+ companies worldwide and 7 interface languages, but publishes no country-eligibility filter [V] — [jobcopilot.com/pricing](https://jobcopilot.com/pricing/); [noxjobs](https://noxjobs.com/compare/best-ai-auto-apply-tools-ranked-honest-comparison-2026). One JobCopilot reviewer complains about jobs with pay far below what they specified, so salary filtering is unreliable [I] — [Trustpilot](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/jobcopilot.com)
- LoopCV is the only tool found that finds recruiter emails and sends outreach, but it does so automatically from templates. It is described as "primarily EU-focused" [C] — [scoutify](https://scoutify.com/blog/loopcv-review/)
- Massive offers visa-sponsorship filters, which are US/UK oriented and not remote-from-Pakistan eligibility [C] — [noxjobs](https://noxjobs.com/compare/best-ai-auto-apply-tools-ranked-honest-comparison-2026)
- Closest non-agent options for Pakistan eligibility: Himalayas has location and timezone filters and a "remote jobs you can do from Pakistan" page with free AI career tools — [himalayas.app/jobs/countries/pakistan](https://himalayas.app/jobs/countries/pakistan). An Apify actor reads 8 remote job boards and labels each job worldwide, regional or US/EU-only by the user's country, with Pakistan listed (a scraper, not a finished app) — [Apify](https://apify.com/yash_harkawat/remote-jobs-eligibility-aggregator). DailyRemote's premium tier claims AI auto-apply and lists Pakistan-eligible roles [V] — [dailyremote](https://dailyremote.com/remote-jobs-in-pakistan)
- Autofill copilots (Simplify, Huntr) work on ATS career sites. I found no evidence that they support Google Forms specifically [C] — [noxjobs](https://noxjobs.com/compare/best-ai-auto-apply-tools-ranked-honest-comparison-2026); [huntr.co/pricing](https://huntr.co/pricing)

### Inferences
- Paying for a commercial tool would at best complement Shortlist. It would not replace it. The Pakistan-specific parts are exactly what no product covers: pasted or screenshotted LinkedIn recruiter posts, Google Forms, a Pakistan/India eligibility filter, a USD pay floor, and email drafts instead of sends.
- A sensible complement would be a free autofill copilot (Huntr free or Simplify free) for ATS forms on international roles, while Shortlist handles discovery, eligibility, emails and Google Forms. Himalayas or remote boards with country filters (Remotive, Working Nomads and similar, outside this note's scope) are better discovery inputs than US-centric agents.

### Gaps
- I did not test whether Simplify, Huntr or JobWizard autofill works on Google Forms. Checking this would take a quick manual test.
- I did not confirm whether paid tools accept Pakistani cards or PKR. Some may block cards or regions.

## Q4. Ideas worth copying into Shortlist

### Takeaway
The most transferable ideas are the stored answer profile behind autofill (Simplify, Huntr), the clipper-to-tracker flow with contacts (Huntr, Teal), per-job match scoring before tailoring (Jobright, career-ops), the "application packet" bundle (Huntr), and a human-approve gate before submit (Nox, career-ops). Shortlist already plans the most important anti-pattern guard, never sending.

### Cited Findings
- **Answer bank or autofill profile**: Simplify+ uses AI answers to custom application questions on top of stored profile autofill [C] — [aceapp](https://aceapp.ai/blog/simplify-review). Huntr offers unlimited autofills from one profile on the free tier [V] — [huntr.co/pricing](https://huntr.co/pricing)
- **Application packet**: Huntr bundles a tailored resume and cover letter per job as a "packet", tracked per job [V] — [huntr.co/pricing](https://huntr.co/pricing)
- **Clipper, tracker, contacts, metrics, map view**: Huntr's Chrome job clipper feeds the tracker, and it adds unlimited contact management, a map view, and "advanced job search metrics" on Pro [V] — [huntr.co/pricing](https://huntr.co/pricing)
- **Match score first, then tailor**: career-ops scores each job 1-5 against the CV before tailoring, and "you press Submit" [open source] — [GitHub career-ops](https://github.com/santifer/career-ops/). Jobright's strength is its match explanations and rich job detail pages [C] — [jobity](https://jobity.io/blog/jobright-review)
- **Human approval gate**: Nox lets the user approve each submission [V] — [noxjobs](https://noxjobs.com/compare/best-ai-auto-apply-tools-ranked-honest-comparison-2026). Reviewers recommend applying through employer career sites with a review step [C] — [resumly](https://resumly.ai/answers/is-auto-apply-safe)
- **Recruiter-email finder plus template**: LoopCV finds the recruiter's email and uses templates [V] — [scoutify](https://scoutify.com/blog/loopcv-review/). Shortlist gets emails from the posts themselves and drafts personalized, fact-checked emails instead.
- **Eligibility labelling**: the Apify actor tags each job worldwide, regional or US/EU-only for the user's country — [Apify](https://apify.com/yash_harkawat/remote-jobs-eligibility-aggregator)
- **Failure modes to avoid** (from complaints): irrelevant roles outside the specified title or pay, silent submission failures, wrong autofill data (blank names, wrong default answers), and no visibility into what was actually sent [I] — [Trustpilot JobCopilot](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/jobcopilot.com); [Trustpilot LazyApply](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/lazyapply.com)

### Inferences
- Shortlist should show a confidence or verification flag for each autofilled answer, and leave blank (not guess) any answer that is not in the bank. This directly counters the LazyApply "wrong default answers" complaint.
- Track the funnel per source: discovered, eligible, drafted, sent by user, replied, interview. Also log rejection reasons for filtered jobs (country, India-only, below floor) so filters can be audited. That gives Shortlist the honest results data commercial tools do not publish.
- Store the exact text sent with each application, for example by linking the Gmail draft ID. Commercial tools are criticized for opacity about what they sent.
- A "packet" view per job (score, eligibility verdict, draft email, form answers, status) mirrors Huntr's packet and fits a single-user UI.

### Gaps
- I did not inspect any product's actual UI or answer-bank schema. These ideas come from feature lists and reviews, not hands-on use.
- I found no public data on which tracking metrics actually predict success.
